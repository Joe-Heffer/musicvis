# One Fuzz: Visualiser Brief

Instrumental drill and bass with fuzz-bass garage rock, 140 BPM, E minor. Live Afrobeat-influenced kit under stuttering programmed breakbeats; octave-fuzz bass riff; fretless jazz runs; acid bassline in the verses; glassy arpeggiated synth loop on top; half-time breakdown in the middle; ends on one fuzz chord hit, then silence.

Look: a flat printed page in spot inks with misregistered layers, hand-made against the digital edits. Any frame should work as a print (cover art).

## Palette
| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Paper (off-white) | `#F2EDE4` |
| Fluorescent pink | `#FF48B0` |
| Teal | `#00838A` |
| Black | `#1B1B1D` |
Inks multiply over the paper like real overprint (pink over teal = dark overprint). Flat inks and halftone density only: no glow, no gradients, no motion blur.

## Audio mapping
- Live polyrhythmic kit: each hit stamps a block of halftone dots into a page grid (kick = black, rim/hat = teal/pink)
- Breakbeat stutters / drill edits: page shreds into strips that slide back into place one beat later
- Fuzz-octave bass riff: solid ink floods the frame on the drops
- Arp synth loop: a repeating stencilled motif that steps along on the 16ths
- Fretless bass runs: one continuous pen line wanders and leaves a drawn trail
- Acid bassline: slow teal halftone tint breathing under the verses
- Misregistration: each ink layer re-pulls per bar and jolts on heavy kicks
- Pops: strong kicks slam a coarse black halftone plate, strong snares print a torn teal band across the page (both gone within a few frames); inside a black flood they print as knockouts
