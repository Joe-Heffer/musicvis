**Title:** Glitch Metal Instrumental — Abstract Audio-Reactive Visualiser

Instrumental glitch metal with pop melodies. Rigid, machine-like riffing against a bright, singable synth hook; the visuals keep the two ideas apart in the verses and fuse them in the chorus.

A cold steel grid fragments and reassembles with the riff, snare stutters become frame repeats and datamosh smears, and a single unbroken ribbon of light traces the synth melody. In the chorus the grid dissolves into depth and the ribbon blooms into the full palette.

Palette: near-black #0A0B0F, steel #2A2E38 / #8A93A3, flash #F4F6FA, hook magenta #FF2E93, glitch cyan #19E3FF, chorus violet #7A3CFF.

Made with: Demucs (stem separation), librosa (analysis), HyperFrames (HTML/WebGL render), ffmpeg, Claude Code. 16:9, 30 fps.
